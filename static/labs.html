Wave Interference & Beat Frequency Lab

Explore acoustic and mathematical wave superposition: y(t) = y₁(t) + y₂(t). When two continuous waves with close frequencies \(f_1\) and \(f_2\) interfere, periodic amplitude pulsing occurs at the exact beat frequency \(f_{\text{beat}} = |f_1 - f_2|\).

Sampling & Aliasing Laboratory

Explore continuous signal discretization and Nyquist-Shannon aliasing: f_N = F_s / 2. When input signal frequency \(f\) exceeds the Nyquist limit \(F_s / 2\), the sampled points fold into the baseband range, creating an apparent lower-frequency alias waveform.

Fourier Series & Harmonics Laboratory

Convolution & Filtering Laboratory

Explore linear time-invariant (LTI) signal filtering: y[n] = \sum_k x[k] h[n-k] and y(t) = \int x(\tau) h(t-\tau) d\tau. Observe how signals combine through flip, shift, overlap, multiplication, and integration.
